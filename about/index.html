ABOUT FRANKIE

Frankie is a versatile vocalist and performer specializing in opera, musical theatre, with a classical repertoire.

Education

Upcoming graduate of the University of Central Florida (UCF) with a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Musical Theatre Performance.

Performance Highlights

  • Performed alongside Andrea Bocelli in his Valentine’s Day Concert in Miami, Florida
  • Featured in operas with the Miami Classical Music Festival, including Massenet’s Cendrillon, Bizet’s Carmen, and Monteverdi’s L’Orfeo
  • TheatreUCF credits include Sweeney Todd (Ensemble, Johanna Understudy), Working (Swing), and Legally Blonde

Teaching

Frankie is an enthusiastic and patient voice teacher who loves working with students of all ages and skill levels. She has served as a teaching assistant for CFCArts’ “Upbeat!” Program and provided private voice lessons to children at The Christ School in Orlando.

She is also fluent in Spanish!

Available for live performances, singing gigs, and private voice lessons. Contact for bookings and inquiries.
